In incident INC-2024-047, a hydraulic press operator detected an unexpected pressure spike of 340 bar, exceeding the standard operating threshold by 60 bar due to a partial blockage in the hydraulic return line caused by metal shavings. The issue was mitigated by initiating an emergency stop before any failure occurred; however, it led to a production halt lasting 47 minutes. The inline filter had surpassed its recommended service interval of 500 hours by 123 hours. No injuries were reported. As corrective actions, all hydraulic inline filters on Assembly Line B are being replaced, and additional safety measures including updated equipment checklists and training sessions have been planned to prevent recurrence.
