Based on the report, I have identified two root causes of the manufacturing safety incident:

**Root Cause 1: Planned Preventive Maintenance Schedule Overdue**

Explanation: The planned preventive maintenance schedule for the ventilation fans was overdue by three weeks. This delay in maintenance likely contributed to the failure of one of the extract ventilation fans, which reduced air extraction capacity by approximately 50 percent. If the fan had been properly maintained, it is possible that it would have continued to function correctly during the spray painting session.

**Root Cause 2: Inadequate Pre-Use Check Procedure**

Explanation: The pre-use check relies on operator observation rather than quantitative measurement, which means that the failed ventilation fan was not detected prior to commencement of the spray painting session. If a quantitative measurement had been taken, the failure would have been identified, and corrective action could have been taken before the incident occurred.

These two root causes are interconnected, as the overdue maintenance likely contributed to the fan failure, which in turn led to inadequate ventilation in the spray booth.
