### Risk Level Classification

**Severity:** Medium  
**Likelihood of Recurrence:** Low to Medium  

#### Justification:
- **Severity:**
  - The incident involved a significant deviation from the standard operating pressure (340 bar instead of the usual 280 bar), which could have led to serious equipment damage or failure had it not been promptly addressed.
  - No injuries were reported, indicating that no direct harm occurred. However, the potential for injury exists due to high-pressure hydraulic systems, where uncontrolled pressurization can lead to ejection of parts or fluid, causing severe physical injuries.
  
- **Likelihood of Recurrence:**
  - The root cause was identified as a partial blockage in the hydraulic return line caused by accumulated metal shavings and an inline filter that exceeded its service interval. Corrective actions are already underway to address these issues:
    - Replacing all filters on Assembly Line B.
    - Updating the pre-shift equipment checklist to include hydraulic pressure baseline checks.
    - Reviewing and updating the hydraulic filter replacement schedule across all production lines.
    - Conducting a toolbox talk on hydraulic system hazard awareness for operators.
  - These corrective actions, if implemented effectively, will significantly reduce the likelihood of recurrence. However, without full implementation and monitoring, there remains some risk.

#### Conclusion:
The incident is classified as **Medium Severity** due to the potential for significant equipment damage or personal injury had it not been for the quick response by the operator. The **Likelihood of Recurrence** is assessed as Low to Medium because while corrective actions are being taken, proper implementation and adherence to new protocols will determine long-term risk reduction effectiveness.
