The incident involved the accidental dropping of two rubber packing components from a suspended cradle at level 22 during aluminium facade installation works. The severity was moderate; although items fell into a public access area where vehicles were parked, no damage to property or injuries occurred. The immediate cause was the absence of a mandatory pre-use inspection checklist for securing materials on the cradle platform and insufficient hazard awareness among crew members. As corrective actions, a new inspection checklist has been implemented, along with requirements for secure material storage, enhanced safety briefings, daily inspections, and revisions to operational procedures and risk assessments.
