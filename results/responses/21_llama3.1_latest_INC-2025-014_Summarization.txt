Here is a 3-5 sentence summary of the manufacturing safety incident report:

The incident type was an electrical shock, classified as critical due to failure of a procedure to identify all energy sources. The technician received a mild electric shock while working on a control panel that had been reported as de-energised and locked out, but actually still contained a live secondary circuit. The immediate cause was the outdated wiring diagram and inadequate lockout/tagout procedure, which did not require verification of live circuits after lockout application. As a result of the incident, corrective actions were taken to update control panel diagrams, revise lockout/tagout procedures, label power supply units on panels, provide refresher training for electrical maintenance staff, and review recent panel modifications.
