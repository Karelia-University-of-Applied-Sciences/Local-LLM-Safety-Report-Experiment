The incident (Report ID: INC-2024-058) involved a forklift operator who sustained a sprained left wrist and minor lacerations after attempting to manually reposition a misaligned pallet on the forks without securing the forklift or obtaining assistance, leading to a fall on a wet floor. The severity was moderate as the operator was unable to work for four days. The immediate cause was the unsafe manual handling procedure combined with the absence of absorbent matting and warning signs at the loading bay entrance. Corrective actions include installing permanent absorbent matting, revising training programs, enhancing pre-shift briefings on slip hazards, and conducting awareness talks on slip hazards for all warehouse staff.
