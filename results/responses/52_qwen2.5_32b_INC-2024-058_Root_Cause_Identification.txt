The report ID INC-2024-058 describes a manufacturing safety incident where a forklift operator was injured due to several root causes:

1. **Operator Unaware of Procedural Violations**: The forklift operator attempted to manually reposition the misaligned pallet without assistance and without securing the forklift, which is against established procedures. This lack of awareness led to unsafe behavior that contributed directly to the accident by placing the operator in a hazardous position where manual handling restrictions should have been observed.

2. **Absence of Wet Floor Warning System or Absorbent Matting**: The presence of water on the floor due to rainwater being tracked inside from outside created a slippery condition at the loading bay entrance. There were no absorbent matting or warning signs in place to mitigate this hazard, which led to the operator losing footing and falling. This absence directly contributed to the slip and fall incident.

3. **Pre-shift Briefing Oversight**: The pre-shift briefing did not include information on weather-related slip hazards. As a result, the operator was not briefed on potential risks associated with wet conditions that could lead to slips or falls, further contributing to unsafe working conditions due to inadequate preparation and risk awareness.

Each of these root causes contributed significantly to the incident by creating an environment where safety protocols were bypassed, and the physical workspace did not adequately mitigate known hazards. The corrective actions aim to address these issues by improving both procedural adherence and environmental management in hazardous work areas.
