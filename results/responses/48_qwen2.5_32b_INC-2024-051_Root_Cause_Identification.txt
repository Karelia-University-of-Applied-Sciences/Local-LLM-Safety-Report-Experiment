Based on the information provided in Report ID: INC-2024-051, there are several root causes identified that contributed to the manufacturing safety incident:

1. **Lack of Proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** The technician was wearing standard nitrile gloves instead of the required chemical-resistant extended-cuff gloves. This inadequacy in PPE led to exposure when a small quantity of solvent splashed onto the forearms above the cuff line of the nitrile gloves, which were not designed for such chemicals.

2. **Stock Depletion and Lack of Replenishment:** The report indicates that chemical-resistant extended-cuff gloves had been depleted at the workstation without being restocked in time. This depletion meant there was no appropriate PPE available to safely handle the solvent, leading the technician to use standard nitrile gloves instead.

3. **Absence of Minimum Stock Level Alerts for PPE:** There was no system or alert mechanism in place to indicate when critical safety items like chemical-resistant extended-cuff gloves were running low. This absence resulted in the depletion going unnoticed until an incident occurred, potentially affecting multiple handling sessions if not addressed promptly.

4. **Proceeding with Inadequate Safety Measures:** The technician chose to proceed with the solvent transfer task using only the available standard nitrile gloves rather than halting work due to a lack of proper PPE. This decision contributed significantly to the exposure and subsequent skin irritation, as it ignored established safety protocols regarding appropriate protective measures.

Each root cause directly influenced the incident by undermining the overall safety protocol designed for handling chemicals at this workstation. The failure to maintain adequate supplies of specific PPE items compounded with inadequate oversight and a lack of adherence to strict safety procedures collectively resulted in an unsafe work environment where chemical exposure could occur, causing injury despite being minor in this instance.
