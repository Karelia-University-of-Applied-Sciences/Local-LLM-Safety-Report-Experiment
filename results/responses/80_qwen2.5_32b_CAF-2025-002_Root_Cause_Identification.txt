The root causes of the manufacturing safety incident described in Report ID: CAF-2025-002 can be identified as:

1. **Insufficient Task Risk Assessment**: The task risk assessment did not adequately address the specific hazards associated with manually transporting large cladding sheets via internal staircases. This contributed to the incident because it failed to identify and mitigate risks unique to this particular task, such as the possibility of a sheet slipping during transport.

2. **Lack of Mechanical Handling Aid or Alternative Route**: Despite the size and weight of the panels making manual staircase transport inherently high risk, no mechanical handling aid or alternative route was identified or implemented before the operation began. This contributed significantly to the incident because using appropriate equipment or choosing safer routes could have prevented the need for risky manual transportation.

These root causes highlight a failure in properly planning and executing the task, leading to an environment where accidents were more likely to occur despite operatives wearing full PPE (personal protective equipment).
